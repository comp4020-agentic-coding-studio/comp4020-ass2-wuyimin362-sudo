Open your [opening record](/records/) and read question 2 — the one sentence
beginning "I am buying it because".

A position earns you money in exactly one way: the thing you own hands you cash,
or somebody pays you more for it than you paid. The first is a **cash flow** —
rent, a dividend, interest, a share of profits. The second requires a buyer, and
the buyer's willingness is a fact about them, not about the thing.

Most of the sentences written in week 1 describe neither. They describe a
narrative: what the thing is going to become, who is paying attention to it,
what it is obviously going to do next. A narrative is not a cash flow and it is
not nothing — it is a real reason prices move, because it is what the next buyer
is buying too. It is simply a reason that can stop without anything happening to
the underlying thing at all.

When that is your only reason, you have no way to tell the difference between the
story ending and the story pausing, because you were never tracking anything that
generates a number on its own. Week 4 named how you acted. This week names why
you thought you were entitled to.

Bring your week 1 sentence to the session. We are going to sort the room's
sentences into cash flows and narratives, unedited, and the sort is usually
lopsided.
