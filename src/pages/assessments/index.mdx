Two pieces of work. The first accounts for one loss; the second decomposes the
whole semester.

Neither is marked on your outcome. A student who finished ahead and a student who
lost everything are read the same way, against the same question: can you show,
from your own numbers, which mechanism took which dollar? A loss of $4 you can
account for to the cent is worth more here than a loss of $400 you cannot, and a
gain is worth nothing at all as evidence — which is the argument the last week of
the course makes at length.

Both read from the same two documents you keep from the first session onwards,
and the formats are on the [records page](/records/).
