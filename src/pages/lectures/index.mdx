Each week owns exactly one named way to lose money, and that name is the week's
whole job. If a paragraph would sit equally well in another week, it has been cut
from both.

Six of the mechanisms you can commit deliberately, with no cooperation from the
market: cost drag, concentration, return chasing, story, overfitting, loss
aversion. These are the ones the assessments can ask you to evidence, because
they are available to everyone in any market. The other five — leverage,
illiquidity, correlation collapse, counterparty risk, and ruin itself — have to
be handed to you, or in the case of leverage are refused to you by the
[bounded-loss policy](/policies/). Those are taught and not assessed, and if a
semester hands them to nobody, nobody's mark changes.

Week 12 carries week 1's mechanism and week 1's slides, unchanged. That is the
course closing its loop rather than a gap in the schedule.
